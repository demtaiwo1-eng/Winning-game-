**Not approved for sale. Not legal advice. Must be reviewed and completed by qualified counsel and the verified rights holder.**

## 1. Parties and effective date

This proposed licence is between the verified rights holder identified in the final checkout/contract (the Licensor) and the person or legal entity purchasing the EPHINTA source package (the Licensee). It takes effect only when the final version is accepted and payment is successfully verified.

## 2. Licensed materials

The final agreement must identify the exact release tag, commit SHA, package checksum, files included and excluded, and all third-party components governed by their own licences. Third-party components remain subject to their original licences; this document cannot override them.

## 3. Proposed grant — requires owner decision

The Licensor may grant one named purchaser a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to access, use, inspect and modify the identified EPHINTA source for the Licensee's own business or projects. The final agreement must specify whether the licence permits production deployments, number of entities/clients, contractor access, affiliates, and internal redistribution.

## 4. Restrictions — requires legal approval

The final agreement should address resale, public redistribution, sublicensing, publishing source code, creating competing source-code packages, removing notices, transferring access, and sharing credentials. Do not assume these restrictions are enforceable until counsel approves the wording and applicable law.

## 5. Price and payment

Proposed price: **US$60 one-time** for the specifically described source package. Taxes, currency conversion, payment fees, invoice requirements and refund rules must be displayed before checkout. Access is granted only after server-side verification of successful payment.

## 6. No included services unless expressly stated

The source package does not automatically include hosting, deployment, custom domains, provider accounts, payment processing accounts, credentials, customer data, production databases, mobile signing keys, app-store accounts, support, maintenance, updates, music catalogues or third-party assets beyond their respective licences.

## 7. Warranty, liability, support and refunds

These provisions require jurisdiction-specific legal review and must be completed before sale. Do not publish placeholder promises or broad exclusions without counsel's approval.

## 8. Termination and access

The final agreement must define material breach, notice/cure periods, termination effects, access revocation and any rights that survive termination. Refund and chargeback handling must match the checkout provider's terms and applicable consumer law.

## 9. Governing law and disputes

**To be completed by counsel** after confirming the seller's legal identity, place of business, customer markets and applicable consumer-protection rules.

## 10. Acceptance

The final agreement must be presented to the buyer before payment, versioned and stored with the order record. A checked box and timestamp should record acceptance. This draft must not be used as the checkout terms.
