## Known Git contributors

Git history contains contributions under `demtaiwo1-eng` and `deytaiwo2`. The repository owner previously described `deytaiwo2` as a collaborator. A collaborator relationship and Git commit authorship do not, by themselves, establish that all copyright has been assigned to EPHINTA or the business that will sell it.

Before claiming exclusive ownership, retain a signed agreement from each contributor that:
1. Identifies the contributor and the legal rights-holding entity.
2. Assigns relevant economic copyright/IP rights to the entity, or grants an explicit, perpetual, worldwide, transferable, sublicensable commercial licence sufficient to operate, modify, distribute, and sell EPHINTA.
3. Covers source code, documentation, database schemas, designs, and contributed assets.
4. Records any pre-existing code or third-party materials that are excluded from the assignment.
5. Confirms that the contributor had authority to provide the rights and discloses employer/client obligations.

Do not publish private contributor agreements or personal addresses in this repository. Keep signed agreements in the owner's private legal records.
