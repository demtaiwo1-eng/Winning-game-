Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions.

What is EPHINTA?

EPHINTA is a connected platform for event discovery, planning, coordination and supported event-management workflows.

Which portal should I use?

Choose the portal that matches your role. Access to restricted management features depends on your authenticated account permissions.

Can I manage payments on the platform?

Payment actions and records depend on the capabilities enabled for your account. Always rely on confirmed server-side transaction status.

Can I use EPHINTA on a phone or tablet?

This blueprint adapts to desktop, tablet and mobile screen sizes. The production application should be tested on supported browsers and devices.

How do I get support?

Use the official support or contact option provided by the live application. Do not send passwords or payment credentials through support messages.
